## Which Best Western promo codes actually work

There's no universal public discount code for Best Western. What exists instead falls into four groups, and only the first three apply to most leisure travelers:

1. **Rate codes for qualifying groups.** Code GOV is for US government employees and military personnel. AAA members get a standard discount (show your membership card at check-in). AARP members 50+ get seniors discounts at participating properties. These are entered in the same "Corporate / Promo Code" field during booking.
2. **Corporate agreement codes.** If your employer has a negotiated agreement with Best Western, you'll receive a numeric or alphanumeric code from them. You can't look these up publicly — they come through your company.
3. **Best Western Rewards member pricing.** Log in at BestWestern.com before searching and member rates apply automatically. These typically run 5% or more below public pricing, per third-party guides to the program.
4. **Seasonal and regional offers.** Best Western runs weekend and seasonal packages with add-ons like free breakfast or parking. In Europe, the Early Web Special gives 15% to 30% off if you book at least 21 days before your stay.

If you're not a government employee, AAA member, AARP member, or corporate traveler, your realistic levers are member pricing, seasonal offers, and cash back. That's still a meaningful stack — it just doesn't come from typing a random code into a box.

## Where to enter a Best Western promo code

The code field is on the search form, not at checkout — a detail that trips up a lot of people who go looking for it after picking their room. The steps:

1. Go to BestWestern.com and search your destination and dates.
2. Expand "Special Rates" or "Corporate / Promo Code" in the search form.
3. Enter your code and click Search.
4. Select your room — the discounted rate should display automatically.

If a code doesn't produce a discounted rate, it either isn't valid for that property and date range or it isn't valid at all. Best Western's central reservations line in the US is 1-800-WESTERN (1-800-937-8376); an agent can apply a corporate rate manually and tell you whether your code works for that specific hotel and those dates.
